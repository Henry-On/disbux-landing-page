Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions that might be of interest
Are We Any Different from Fintech's like Opay and PalmPay?
Yes, We are different, unlike traditional FinTech's like Opay and Palmpay, we do not process inter banks transfer. Our payment processing is handled by third party company, MoniePoint (Through their Monnify payment gateway). We do not have any direct interactions with Nigeria Interbank Services (NIBS), CBN and other financial institutions that are involved with processing of payment within the Nigerian regulatory bodies.
Why Don't We Process Payment to all banks
We are yet to obtain the neccessary documents need to process interbank transfers. We operate internally within our App and process payments to other banks with Monnify payment gateway
Is there still any core feature(s) yet to implemented on the App
Yes, so many features are yet to be implemented. We are at early stage and still building. We will be pushing out updates regularly as our teams of developers work towards implementing more of them
